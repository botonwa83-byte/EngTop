## 中文

本 App 为单机英语学习工具，全部题目与解析内置在安装包中，无需注册、无需登录、无服务器交互。

- 主要功能：主线七关（语法填空 / 完形 / 七选五 / 阅读 / 应用文 / 读后续写 / 听力）的练习与得分点模型讲解；提分雷达、错因诊断、估分器、考点图谱、句式库、能力训练营、错题本，以及读后续写工坊与应用文工坊。
- 免费范围：主线前 3 关（语法填空 / 完形 / 七选五）全部题目；提分驾驶舱、估分器、提分雷达、考点图谱、句式库、词汇专项、错题本、能力训练营；两个工坊每个体裁/主题第 1 个场景免费预览。
- 付费内容：完整版解锁（非消耗型内购，产品 ID `com.engtopapp.app.full_unlock`，¥22 一次性买断），解锁后开放主线第 4–7 关、工坊全部场景与写作教练。
- 内购测试路径：安装后进入「更多」页 →「解锁完整版」，可看到付费墙；也可从主线第 4 关的锁定入口进入。沙盒账号可直接购买或点击「恢复购买」。
- 无第三方 SDK、无广告、无分析、无用户数据上传。听力题使用系统语音合成（AVSpeechSynthesizer）朗读，不录音、不上传。

## English

EngTop is an offline English learning app. All questions and explanations are bundled in the app; there is no sign-up, no login and no server communication.

- Features: seven main levels (grammar fill-in, cloze, seven-out-of-five, reading, applied writing, continuation writing, listening) with scoring-point models, plus gain radar, error-cause diagnosis, score estimator, knowledge map, phrase bank, ability gym, mistake book and two writing workshops.
- Free tier: the first three main levels in full, plus gain cockpit, score estimator, gain radar, knowledge map, phrase bank, vocabulary drills, mistake book and ability gym; the first scenario of each writing workshop is free.
- Paid tier: one-time non-consumable unlock (product ID `com.engtopapp.app.full_unlock`, CNY 22) that opens levels 4-7, all workshop scenarios and the writing coach.
- IAP test path: open the app → "更多" tab → "解锁完整版" to see the paywall, or tap any locked level from level 4. Use a sandbox account to purchase, or tap "恢复购买".
- No third-party SDKs, no ads, no analytics, no data uploaded. Listening items are read aloud with the system speech synthesizer; nothing is recorded or uploaded.
